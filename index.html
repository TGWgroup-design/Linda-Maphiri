Her story

One woman. Many crowns.

Linda Maphiri is a businesswoman, a woman of faith and a mother who has made it her life’s work to help other women rise. She leads a growing group of businesses across plant hire, petroleum and retail, and she founded Queening and Winning, a soul clinic where women come to heal, grow and win together.

When she takes the stage, her message is simple and personal: believe in yourself, embrace your potential, and discover your true purpose. It is the same message she lives out at home, in her businesses and in the communities she serves.
